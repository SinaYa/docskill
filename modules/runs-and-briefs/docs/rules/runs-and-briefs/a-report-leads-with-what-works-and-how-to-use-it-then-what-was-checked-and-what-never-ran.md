# A report leads with what works and how to use it, then what was checked and what never ran

- First what works and how to use it: practical steps (how to open it, what to click or type),
  then details of the tool.
- Then what was checked and how, and what never ran, said plainly. What was not verified is
  never implied to have been.
- Then the decisions taken that the user may want to review, and any question last.
