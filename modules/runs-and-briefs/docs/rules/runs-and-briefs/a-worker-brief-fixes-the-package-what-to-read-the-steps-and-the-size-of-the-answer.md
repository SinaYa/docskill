# A worker brief fixes the package, what to read, the steps and the size of the answer

- You work on one package of the run's plan. Do exactly your package — nothing more. Going beyond it is a cost multiplied over many agents.
- Sections: The project in four lines; Read first (only these, then only your sources).
- **Grep first; read sections, not whole files.**
- Before concluding anything, check whether the user's words or the decided models already answer it. If they do, the answer is obvious: cite it.
- **Decide only what is obvious; research the rest**. Obvious = follows from the user's words or the decided models, with the source cited. Otherwise research first, then conclude.
- Research budget (only when the answer is not obvious): a fixed number of web searches and page fetches per package.
- Research is a source, not authority.
- Stop when you have enough to choose; do not survey exhaustively.
- Step 1 — decide (no edits outside your decision file): the problem, what already answers it, the conclusion, the change per target file, and anything you noticed and did not act on.
- Step 2 — write (only when the lead tells you). Edit only your *Target files*.
- Shared reference files are never target files: list their edits under *Reference edits*. One agent applies them.
- Return to the lead **at most 150 words** after deciding, and **at most 100 words** after writing.
